Teaching & outreach

Teaching assistantships at IIT Bombay were a formal way to help juniors with their coursework — and the fastest way I found to sharpen my own technical communication.

Lab courses

Laboratory work runs through the whole Physics curriculum at IITB. As a lab TA I ran the weekly sessions, walked students through the assignments, helped debug circuits and graded the work.
