Preserving Coulomb blockade in transport spectroscopy of quantum dots by dynamical tunnel-barrier compensation

Sep 2024

D. Shah, A. Author, B. Author, S. Mahapatra

Journal name, volume, page (2024)

Silicon Quantum Computing Lab, IIT Bombay · Supervisor: Prof. Suddhasatta Mahapatra

In surface-gated quantum dots the plunger gate also modulates the tunnel barriers, which limits the range over which Coulomb blockade survives. Dynamically tuning the barriers to compensate for that capacitive cross-talk preserves blockade across a much wider gate-voltage window, extending the usable range of transport spectroscopy.
