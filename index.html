Hello there

Devashish Shah

PhD candidate, Institute of Science and Technology Austria

I am a PhD candidate at ISTA working on quantum transport, superconductivity and topological phenomena in low-dimensional devices. I am interested in how these effects can be turned into something useful — for quantum information processing and for precision sensing.

Before this I read Engineering Physics at IIT Bombay, where I worked on silicon quantum dots and on cantilever-based force metrology.

A little more

I have enjoyed physics and taking electronics apart for as long as I can remember, and most of what I do now is a continuation of that. During my undergraduate degree I was a teaching assistant at IIT Bombay for four courses, which I found as rewarding as the lab work. Away from the fridge I sketch and play squash.

My coursework and laboratory training covered electronics, solid-state physics, nuclear physics, optics and spectroscopy. Alongside it I worked in the Silicon Quantum Computing Lab, where I learned most of what I know about low-temperature measurement.
